# Sun Constraints

## Function Description

Sun Constraints are a group of constraints that filter accesses according to the relative positions of the Sun, the Moon, and specified celestial bodies, and are used to avoid unfavorable lighting conditions and celestial-body geometry relationships.

The individual Sun constraints are independent. A given time satisfies the Sun constraints only when all enabled conditions are met.

::: info Related Documents
For the geometric definitions, reference coordinate systems, and computational models of each condition, see [Theory - Sun Constraints](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md).
:::

## Parameter Descriptions

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg|Range: [-90°, 90°]" name="Sun Elevation Angle" desc="The angle between the direction from the analysis object to the Sun and the OXY plane of its body-fixed frame. It is positive when the Sun is above the reference plane and negative when the Sun is below it." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

![Schematic of the Sun elevation angle](../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/03-约束配置/media/02-太阳约束/sun-elevation.svg)

- After selecting 【Min】, only periods when the Sun elevation angle is no less than the specified value are retained;
- After selecting 【Max】, only periods when the Sun elevation angle is no greater than the specified value are retained;
- When both are enabled, periods when the Sun elevation angle falls within the specified range are retained.

> **For example:** Min set to 10 and Max set to 60 means that only periods when the Sun elevation angle is between 10° and 60° are retained.

> **Note**: The Sun elevation angle indicates how far the Sun is above or below the reference plane. It is not the angle between the access line of sight and the Sun direction.

For the definition of the reference plane and the elevation angle and the calculation method, see [Sun and Lunar Elevation Angles](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md#elevation).

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg|Range: [-90°, 90°]" name="Sun Gnd Elev Angle" desc="The angle between the direction from the reference ground object to the Sun and its local horizontal plane. It is positive when the Sun is above the local horizontal plane and negative when the Sun is below it." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

Select 【Min】 or 【Max】 and enter the corresponding angle. For example:

> - Min set to -6 means that only periods when this angle is no less than -6° are retained.
> - Max set to 10 means that only periods when the Sun Gnd Elev Angle at the reference ground location is no greater than 10° are retained.

![Schematic of the Sun ground elevation angle: the vertex is at the reference ground object G and the reference plane is the local horizontal plane of G; the Sun direction is positive above the local horizontal plane and negative below it](../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/03-约束配置/media/02-太阳约束/sun-horizon-elevation.svg)

> **Note**: When using this constraint, identify the ground object used as the reference for the calculation. The Sun elevation angle at a ground location must not be confused with the Sun elevation angle at the space-based platform itself.

For the coordinate system and angle definitions, see [Sun Gnd Elev Angle](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md#sun-ground-elevation).

</Entry>

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg|Range: [-90°, 90°]" name="Lunar Elevation Angle" desc="The angle between the direction from the analysis object to the Moon and the OXY plane of its body-fixed frame. It is positive when the Moon is above the reference plane and negative when the Moon is below it." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

It uses the same reference plane as 【Sun Elevation Angle】 (the OXY plane of the analysis object's body-fixed frame), and the Min/Max evaluation is also the same; only the Sun direction is replaced by the Moon direction.

> **Note**: This constraint limits how far the Moon is above or below the reference plane. To constrain the angle between the access line of sight and the Moon direction, use 【Lunar Exclusion Angle】 in the 【Line Of Sight】 group.

For the definition of the reference plane and the calculation method, see [Sun and Lunar Elevation Angles](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md#elevation).

</Entry>

<Entry name="Additional Central Body Obstruction" desc="Determines whether the line-of-sight path between the analysis object and the associated object passes through the body of the selected celestial body." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

After selecting 【Use】 in this group, select the bodies to consider in the 【Available Central Bodies】 list on the left and click the right arrow to move them into the 【Assigned Central Bodies】 list on the right. During the calculation, ATK checks obstruction by each assigned body: **if any assigned body obstructs the line-of-sight path between the analysis object and the associated object, the constraint is not satisfied at that time**.

To remove a body, select it in 【Assigned Central Bodies】 and click the left arrow. Clear 【Use】 in this group to disable the whole set of additional obstruction conditions.

![Additional Central Body Obstruction panel](../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/03-约束配置/media/02-太阳约束/image-20260917174130245.png)

::: warning Note
This constraint only determines whether the line-of-sight path is obstructed by a celestial body. It does not determine whether the angle between the line of sight and the body direction is too small; for the latter, see 【Third Body Exclusion Angle】. The two cannot replace each other.
:::

For the geometric principles, see [Additional Central Body Obstruction](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md#central-body-obstruction).

</Entry>

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Third Body Exclusion Angle" desc="The angle between the access line of sight and the direction of the specified celestial body. Entries can be created one by one per body, each with its own threshold." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

With the analysis object as the vertex, the body direction as the axis, and the threshold angle α_min of that body as the half-angle, a **keep-out cone** can be constructed. When the access line of sight falls inside the cone (that is, the actual angle α < α_min), it is too close to the body direction and the constraint is not satisfied at that time.

![Schematic of the third body exclusion angle: a keep-out cone with the body direction as the axis and α_min as the half-angle; a line of sight inside the cone does not satisfy the constraint, while one outside it does](../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/03-约束配置/media/02-太阳约束/body-exclusion-angle.svg)

Select 【Use】 in this group, click 【Add】 to create an exclusion entry, and set the corresponding celestial body and angle. To remove an existing entry, select it and click 【Remove】; click 【Remove All】 to clear all entries in this group. Clear 【Use】 to disable the group settings.

The difference from 【Solar Exclusion Angle】 and 【Lunar Exclusion Angle】 is that this constraint can create entries for any celestial body one by one, each with its own threshold, whereas those two are fixed shortcuts for the Sun and the Moon.

| Interface Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Celestial Body | The celestial body associated with the entry. |
| Angle (deg) | The exclusion angle threshold of that body, that is, the minimum allowable angle between the access line of sight and the direction of that body. |

![Third Body Exclusion Angle panel](../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/03-约束配置/media/02-太阳约束/image-20260917174843492.png)

::: warning Note
This condition only compares the direction angle. It does not determine whether the access line of sight is obstructed by the body itself; for the latter, see 【Additional Central Body Obstruction】. The two cannot replace each other.
:::

For the general angle model, see [Line Of Sight Exclusion Angles](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md#exclusion-angle).

</Entry>

### Line Of Sight

The following two conditions are evaluated by the angle between the **access line of sight** (the direction from the analysis object to the associated object) and the direction of the celestial body; the angle unit is deg for both.

![Line Of Sight group: Solar Exclusion Angle and Lunar Exclusion Angle](../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/03-约束配置/media/02-太阳约束/image-20260918115943900.png)

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Solar Exclusion Angle" desc="The angle between the access line of sight and the Sun direction. When the actual angle is less than the specified threshold, the constraint is not satisfied at that time." level="4" objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

Select this option and enter an angle. If the actual angle between the access line of sight and the Sun direction is **less than** this value, the line of sight falls inside the keep-out cone with the Sun direction as its axis, and the constraint is not satisfied at that time.

![Schematic of the solar exclusion angle: a keep-out cone with the Sun direction as the axis and the threshold θ as the half-angle; a line of sight inside the cone does not satisfy the constraint, while one outside it does](../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/03-约束配置/media/02-太阳约束/sun-exclusion-angle.svg)

The evaluation principle is exactly the same as for 【Third Body Exclusion Angle】; the only difference is that the body is fixed to the Sun, so no entries need to be created one by one.

For the general angle model, see [Line Of Sight Exclusion Angles](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md#exclusion-angle).

</Entry>

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Lunar Exclusion Angle" desc="The angle between the access line of sight and the Moon direction. When the actual angle is less than the specified threshold, the constraint is not satisfied at that time." level="4" objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

Select this option and enter an angle. The evaluation principle is exactly the same as for 【Solar Exclusion Angle】; the only difference is that the body is fixed to the Moon.

For the general angle model, see [Line Of Sight Exclusion Angles](../../../04-理论基础/02-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md#exclusion-angle).

</Entry>
