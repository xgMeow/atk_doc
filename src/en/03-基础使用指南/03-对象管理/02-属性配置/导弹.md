# Missile

Missile property settings include: **Basic Properties, 2D Graphics, 3D Graphics, Constraints, and RF**.

- The overall configuration items are essentially the same as those for **Rocket**.
- The only difference is that **Swath settings are not included** in the **2D Graphics** section.
- All other parameters, interfaces, and operations are identical to those in [Rocket Property Settings](./火箭.md), so they are not repeated here.

---

## Constraints

| Property | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Basic](../03-约束配置/01-基本约束.md) | Geometric and line-of-sight conditions that access between the missile and other objects must satisfy (azimuth, elevation, range, propagation delay, altitude, field of view, etc.) |
| [Sun](../03-约束配置/02-太阳约束.md) | Conditions determined by the relative positions of the Sun and the Moon that access between the missile and other objects must satisfy (Sun/Moon elevation angle, exclusion angle, additional central body obstruction, etc.) |
| [Time](../03-约束配置/03-时间约束.md) | Time windows within which access between the missile and other objects must fall (local time/GMT/local solar time/duration/time intervals) |
